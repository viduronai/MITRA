# Indian English script

> Speak your normal Indian English, the way you'd talk to a customer at a front desk.
> No foreign accent, no news-reader tone. Numbers are written the way you'd say them.
> About 70–80 seconds at a natural pace.

## Full script

Good morning, this is Sri Sai Clinic. How may I help you today?

Yes, doctor is available today. We're open Monday to Saturday, from nine in the morning
to eight at night. Sunday is a holiday.

The consultation fee is five hundred rupees, and follow-up within one week is free.

Mm-hmm… okay… right, I understand. Just one second, let me check the slots for you…

So, there's a slot tomorrow evening at six thirty. Otherwise, day after tomorrow at eleven
in the morning is also free. Which one works better for you?

Sure, I'm booking you for tomorrow, six thirty in the evening. Your name is Ravi, and the
number is nine eight seven six five, four three two one zero. Is that correct?

Oh, I'm really sorry the call got disconnected earlier. Sorry for the trouble.

Your appointment is confirmed. I'm sending the details to your WhatsApp right now. If you
need to change anything, just call this same number. Is there anything else I can help
you with?

Okay, thank you so much. Take care, and see you tomorrow!
